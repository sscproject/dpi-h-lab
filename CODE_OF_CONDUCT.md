# Code of Conduct

DPI-H Lab brings together people from ministries of health, regional organisations, standards bodies, universities and industry, across many countries and cultures. We want taking part to be welcoming, respectful and safe for everyone.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

## Our standards

**Do:**

- be kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree
- assume good intent, and remember that many contributors work in a second or third language
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, focused on the work
- credit others' work and ideas

**Don't:**

- harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately
- use sexualised language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances
- publish anyone's private information without their permission
- disrupt discussions or behave in ways that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers listed in [AUTHORS](AUTHORS) privately. Reports are handled in confidence. Maintainers may remove comments, edit content, or temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is unacceptable, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
